# DEPI-R5-Professional-Data-Analyst-project

> **📎 Project Name:** Healthcare Operations & Patient Analytics 360: Multi-Tool Analytics Engine
> 
> **📌 Project Overview:**
>  This project delivers a comprehensive healthcare analytics solution evaluating hospital operational performance across patient demographics, encounter classifications, clinical procedures, payer coverage, and 30-day readmission patterns. Modern healthcare organizations often struggle with fragmented visibility across the end-to-end clinical and financial pipeline—from initial patient admission and insurance payer coverage allocation to procedure execution costs and post-discharge readmission risks. To resolve this, raw operational tables are transformed through an end-to-end data lifecycle: auditing relational integrity, handling logical anomalies and outliers, conducting exploratory data analysis, and delivering interactive business intelligence dashboards.

> **🎯 Project Objectives:**
>* Establish a Unified Relational Schema: Architect and validate a relational model connecting Patients, Encounters, >Procedures, and Payers with strict primary and foreign key integrity.
>* Cleanse & Audit Data Anomalies: Screen records for duplicate encounters, resolve logical missing values, and isolate cost and duration outliers.
>* Measure Operational & Clinical KPIs: Compute critical healthcare benchmarks, including annual encounter volumes, encounter class distributions, zero-payer coverage percentages, and 30-day readmission rates.

> **📦 Project Scope :**
>
> * ***Relational Data Modeling:*** Structuring normalized tables and star-schema dimensional relationships across patient demographics, clinical encounters, therapeutic procedures, and insurance payers.
> * ***Data Preprocessing & Validation:*** Conducting duplicate audits, masking unrecorded stop timestamps to handle hidden nulls, rectifying status inconsistencies, and detecting chronological outliers (StopDate < StartDate).
> * ***Core KPI Engineering:*** Formulating executive healthcare metrics, including Encounter Duration, Average Claim Cost, Payer Coverage Ratio, and 30-Day Readmission Rate.
> * ***Four Independent Analytical Implementations:***
> 
> 1. **Python:** Data manipulation and cleansing with Pandas/NumPy, exploratory statistical analysis, and interactive dashboard development via Streamlit/Plotly.
> 2. **SQL:** Relational schema creation, analytical querying using CTEs, window functions, and business reporting summary views.
> 3. **Tableau:** Visual modeling, calculated fields, dynamic parameters, level-of-detail (LOD) expressions, and geographic patient distribution maps.
> 4. **Power BI:** Star schema modeling, DAX measure creation, interactive cross-filtering, and executive visual reporting.
> 5. **Actionable Business Recommendations:** Translating metrics into operational improvements for resource allocation, insurance claim optimization, and high-risk patient follow-up programs.
